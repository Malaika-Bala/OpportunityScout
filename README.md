# OpportunityScout
Browser agent that finds and compares student opportunities such as hackathons, internships, and competitions.
# OpportunityScout

> **Discover. Compare. Apply.**
>
> A smart opportunity discovery platform built to help students find relevant hackathons, internships, competitions, and scholarships without searching across multiple platforms.

---

## Overview

Students often spend hours searching across different websites to find opportunities that match their interests, skills, eligibility, and deadlines.

**OpportunityScout** aims to simplify this process by bringing opportunity discovery into a single, student-focused interface.

Users can search for opportunities using natural language, apply filters, sort results, and view detailed information about each opportunity.

The current MVP uses curated/demo opportunity data, with a scalable architecture designed for future browser-agent and real-time opportunity discovery capabilities.

---

## Problem Statement

Finding the right student opportunity is often difficult because information is distributed across multiple platforms.

Students need to:

- Search multiple websites
- Check eligibility requirements
- Track application deadlines
- Compare different opportunities
- Determine whether an opportunity is online or offline
- Decide which opportunities are most relevant to them

This fragmented process is time-consuming and can cause students to miss valuable opportunities.

---

## Our Solution

OpportunityScout provides a centralized platform for discovering and evaluating student opportunities.
